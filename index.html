WE ARE HERE TO HELP

HUSTLEHUB Support

Find answers to common questions about your account, jobs, wallet, referrals, and staying safe online.

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Open HUSTLEHUB, select Register, and enter your full name, email address, and a secure password. Use an email address you can access and follow any email confirmation instructions.

How do I log in?

Enter the email address and password you used when registering. Check your details if login fails.

What if I cannot access my account?

Check your internet connection, confirm your login details, and complete email confirmation if required. Never share your password or verification codes with anyone.

Jobs and Opportunities

How do I find jobs?

Log in and open the Jobs page. Browse the available opportunities and read each listing carefully.

How do I apply for a job?

Read the job description and follow the employer's application instructions. If an application link is provided, check that it belongs to a trustworthy destination before submitting your information.

Why can't I see a particular job?

Only published opportunities are shown to job seekers. A listing may have been unpublished, removed, or closed.

Stay alert: Verify job details and employers. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed employment in exchange for unexpected payments.

Account Activation

What does account activation mean?

Activation confirms that an account has met HUSTLEHUB's activation requirements.

What if my account is not activated?

Check your account status and follow the instructions displayed on HUSTLEHUB. If your status does not update, seek help through an official support channel when available.

Important: Do not send money to personal numbers or trust messages from unverified people claiming to represent HUSTLEHUB. Only follow payment instructions displayed through an officially verified payment process.

Wallet and Transactions

Why is my wallet balance KSh 0.00?

Your balance may be zero if no funds have been credited. Check your transaction history for available records.

Can I deposit money right now?

Deposits are currently disabled. The deposit form does not initiate a payment or send an M-Pesa prompt.

Can I withdraw money right now?

Withdrawals are currently disabled. The withdrawal form does not send money to your phone.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The current minimum withdrawal setting is KSh 400. Withdrawals remain unavailable until the service is enabled.

Where can I see my transactions?

Open the Wallet page and scroll to Transaction History. Available wallet transaction records will appear there.

Referrals and Rewards

How do referral rewards work?

HUSTLEHUB's current referral reward is KSh 40 for an eligible referral. The reward should only be credited after the required activation payment has been genuinely verified and the referral meets the platform's rules.

Why haven't I received my reward?

The referral may not yet meet the eligibility requirements, or its payment confirmation may still need processing. Check your wallet and transaction history.

Can I receive the same reward twice?

Rewards are subject to referral verification and duplicate-processing safeguards. A referral should not generate repeated credits for the same eligible payment.

Account and Job Safety

  • Keep your password private and use a strong, unique password.
  • Never share your M-Pesa PIN, password, or verification codes.
  • Verify employers and job details before applying.
  • Be cautious of unrealistic salary promises or guaranteed jobs.
  • Do not pay unverified recruiters who promise employment.
  • Do not share unnecessary personal or financial information.
Protect your personal information. Report suspicious activity through an official HUSTLEHUB support channel when one is available.

Contact Support

If your question is not answered above, prepare a clear description of the problem and the page where it occurred.

Include your registered email address only when contacting an official support channel. Never include your password, M-Pesa PIN, or verification codes.
